Getting paid with Payoneer: a first-time walkthrough

Who it is for
freelancers and small businesses in Pakistan
Time
about 30 minutes, plus waiting for verification
Last checked
25 November 2026

Seedhi baat: signing up for Payoneer is easy. Verification is where most people get stuck. This guide shows the steps in order and the mistakes to avoid.

What Payoneer does

It gives you an online account that can hold money from clients abroad. When you are ready, you withdraw it to your own bank account in Pakistan.

Have these ready first

  • Your CNIC, with a clear photo of the front and back
  • Your address written exactly as it appears on your CNIC and your bank
  • A bank account in your own name
  • One document that shows your name and that same address (see step 4)

The steps

  1. Sign up as an individual. Use your name exactly as it is on your CNIC. A spelling difference can cause problems later.
  2. Enter your address carefully. Type it the same way it appears on your ID and bank records. You may not be able to edit it later in the app, so check before you continue.
  3. Verify your identity. Payoneer asks you to confirm who you are with your ID and a visual check. Use good light and keep the whole card in the frame.
  4. Verify your address. You upload one document that shows your name and the address you entered. In our check, the options were a utility bill, a bank or card statement (or an account confirmation letter), or a tax document. The document does not have to be in English.
  5. Add your bank account. Once your account is approved, add your Pakistani bank account under Withdraw. The account name should match your Payoneer name.
  6. Get paid. Send your client your Payoneer receiving details, or use the Request option to ask for a payment. Never share your password or any code Payoneer sends you.
  7. Withdraw. When money arrives, use Withdraw to send it to your bank. Check the fee and the exchange rate shown before you confirm.

Tip for renters

If your utility bills are in your landlord's name, they will not match yours. A bank statement or account confirmation letter showing your name and address often works instead. If your bank has a different address on file, update it at your branch first, then use that same address in Payoneer.

Common mistakes

  • Typing a different address from the one on your ID or bank
  • Uploading a document that does not show your name
  • Blurry, dark or cropped photos
  • Using a bill that is old or in someone else's name
  • Quoting a client a price before you check your withdrawal fees

What we are not sure about

Payoneer's fees, processing times and accepted documents can change and can differ between accounts. We are not sure what yours will be. Check the fees section in your own account and the Payoneer help center before you rely on any number.
