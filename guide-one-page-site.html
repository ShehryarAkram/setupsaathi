Build a one-page site that explains what you do

Who it is for
freelancers and small businesses with no website yet
Time
about 2 hours for a first version
Last checked
25 November 2026

Chhota start bilkul theek hai. One clear page is better than a big site that is never finished. Its only job is to help a visitor understand what you do and how to reach you.

What goes on the page

  1. A headline that says what you do and who for, in one sentence. For example: "I design simple logos for small shops in Pakistan."
  2. A short paragraph: the problem you solve and how you work.
  3. Your services, as a short list of 3 to 5 items. Keep each one to a line.
  4. Proof, if you have it. Real samples of your work are enough. See the tip below.
  5. How to contact you: an email address and, if you use it for work, a WhatsApp number.
  6. One clear next step, such as "Send me a message to get a quote."

Be honest about proof

Do not invent reviews, client names or results. If you have no clients yet, show samples and label them clearly, for example "Sample project" or "Practice work". Real and small is better than impressive and fake.

Writing tips

  • Use short sentences and plain words.
  • Say what you do in the first line, not your life story.
  • Write for one type of client, not everyone.
  • Use "you" more than "I".

Before you publish

  • Open the page on your phone. Most visitors will.
  • Read it out loud and fix any spelling mistakes.
  • Click every link and send yourself a message from the contact option.
  • Check that your name and contact details are correct.
  • Make sure it loads quickly and the text is easy to read.

Where to put it

A simple one-page site can run on free static hosting. You pay only if you want your own web address (a domain). See our guide on choosing hosting.

What we are not sure about

What works best depends on your skill and your clients. Treat this as a first version, then improve it from the questions people ask you.
