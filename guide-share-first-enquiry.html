Share your page and get your first enquiry

Who it is for
freelancers who have just published a page
Time
a little each day for a few weeks
Last checked
25 November 2026

A page does nothing until people see it. Your first enquiry usually comes from people who already know you, not from strangers. Start close to home and widen slowly.

Where to share it

  • Message people you know who might need your service, or know someone who does. Do this one by one, not in bulk.
  • Add the link to your WhatsApp About, your Facebook and your LinkedIn.
  • Join online groups for your type of work. Read the group rules first and do not spam.
  • Show a sample of your work in a short post and link to your page.
  • If you work on a freelancing platform, check its rules first. Some platforms do not allow you to take clients off the platform.

A simple first message

Keep it short and personal. For example: "Hi [name], I have started offering [service] for [type of client]. If you or anyone you know needs this, here is my page: [link]. Happy to answer any questions."

Ask for introductions

People are often happy to pass your name on if you ask clearly. Ask "do you know anyone who needs this?" instead of "do you need this?"

When your first enquiry arrives

  1. Reply within a day, politely and clearly.
  2. Ask what they need, by when and their budget.
  3. Check they are real. See our guide on what to check before you share payment details.
  4. Agree the work, price and payment in writing before you start.
  5. Start with a small job, and keep records of messages and payments.

Keep a simple list

  • Write down who you messaged, the date, what they said and when to follow up.
  • Follow up once after a few days. Do not keep chasing.

What not to do

  • Do not buy followers, fake reviews or fake testimonials.
  • Do not send the same message to hundreds of strangers.
  • Do not promise results you cannot control.
  • Do not take work from someone who asks for your login details or a fee up front.

Be patient

The first enquiry can take weeks, and some weeks nothing happens. That is normal. Keep sharing, keep improving the page, and learn from every question you get.

What we are not sure about

We cannot say how fast you will get enquiries. It depends on your skill, your market and how many people see your work. Platform rules also change, so check them before you share links.
