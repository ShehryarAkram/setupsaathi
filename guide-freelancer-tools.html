The small set of tools a new freelancer needs

Who it is for
new freelancers and small business owners
Time
about 10 minutes
Last checked
25 November 2026

You do not need twenty tools to start. Think in jobs, then pick one tool for each job. These are common examples, not endorsements.

The jobs you need to cover

  • Getting paid: a way to receive money from clients abroad. See our Payoneer guide.
  • Email: an address you check every day. Start with a clean, professional-looking one, such as your first and last name.
  • Documents and storage: free tools such as Google Docs and Google Drive are enough to begin.
  • Calls and calendar: a free video call tool and a calendar so clients can book time with you.
  • A page that shows your work: one simple page with what you do, examples and how to contact you.
  • Task tracking: a notebook, a spreadsheet or a free task board. Keep it simple.
  • Invoices and agreements: a simple template in a document. Always agree the work and payment in writing first.
  • Basic design: a free plan of a design tool is enough for simple graphics.
  • Security: a password manager, and two-factor protection on your email and payment accounts.

What to skip at first

  • Paid course bundles
  • Expensive all-in-one software suites
  • Tools that need a long contract
  • Anything you cannot explain a use for this week

Three questions before you add a tool

  1. Does it solve a problem I have this week?
  2. Is there a free plan I can start on?
  3. Can I leave later and take my data with me?

What we are not sure about

Free plans, features and prices change, and some tools may work differently in Pakistan. Check the current plan and the sign-up and payment options on each tool's own website.
