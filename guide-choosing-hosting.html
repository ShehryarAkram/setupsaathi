Choosing hosting as a beginner in Pakistan

Who it is for
beginners who want a website
Time
about 15 minutes
Last checked
25 November 2026

Seedhi baat: you may not need to pay for hosting yet. Start with what your website actually has to do.

Do you need paid hosting?

A simple one-page site that shows what you do and how to contact you can run on free static hosting, such as GitHub Pages. The only thing you must buy for your own web address is the domain name, which is a yearly fee.

Paid hosting makes sense when you need things like WordPress, a contact form that sends email, an online store, or software that runs on a server.

The three main types

  • Free static hosting: free, good for simple pages with no server software.
  • Shared hosting: the cheapest paid option. Many sites share one server. Fine for small sites that are just starting.
  • Cloud or VPS hosting: more control and power, but more setup and a higher cost. Usually for sites that have grown.

What to compare before you pay

  • The total cost over three years, not only the first-year offer. Renewal prices are often higher.
  • Payment methods that work for you. Check this before you sign up.
  • The refund policy and how many days you have to ask.
  • Support: live chat, hours and language.
  • Where the servers are, and how fast the site loads for your visitors.
  • Whether backups, an SSL certificate and email are included.
  • Whether you can export your site if you want to leave.

Register your domain in your own account

Buy the domain in your own name and keep the login safe. If your domain is tied to someone else's account, you can lose control of your website.

Common mistakes

  • Paying for several years before you have tested the service
  • Adding extras at checkout that you do not need
  • Looking at the first-year price and ignoring the renewal price
  • Forgetting to turn on two-factor protection for your hosting and domain accounts
